## 03 · H2H context

Use the H2H table: for each league, my rank, my H2H points, this
gameweek's opponent, their rank and the points gap.

For each league answer in one line each:

1. **Stakes.** Is this a six-pointer (opponent within one win of me in the
   standings) or a routine match?
2. **Opponent strength.** Their FPL total versus mine: are they stronger
   overall, and does that change how much variance I should accept this
   week (e.g. a differential captain rather than the template pick)?
3. **Adjustment.** Does the H2H picture change anything from modules 01 or
   02? Usually it should not; say "no change" if so.

Do not speculate about the opponent's squad — the digest does not include
it.
